## F1 — Measurement setup (two panels)

TikZ, `\input`-ed. Panel (a) bench, MCU-only (`TEST_MODE 3`, sensor detached);
panel (b) sensor-in-the-loop, identical wiring for `TEST_MODE 4` (latency) and
`TEST_MODE 5` (energy).

```latex
\caption{Measurement setup. The INA226 ($R_{\mathrm{sh}}=0.1\,\Omega$, VBS tied to
  IN$-$ for load-side bus sensing) measures the full supply current of the target;
  latency is timed in firmware with Timer\_A and reported over UART. In (b) the
  MPU6050 is powered from the measured rail, so the reported power is
  \emph{system} power (MCU + bus + sensor). The LaunchPad's J5 and 3V3 isolation
  jumpers are removed, and IN$+$/IN$-$ must not share a breadboard node. Panels
  (a) and (b) differ only in firmware mode and in whether the sensor is attached;
  latency and energy in (b) therefore refer to the same physical configuration.}
```

## F2 — Real-time budget with the sensor in the loop ⭐

Two panels (100 kHz / 10 kHz I²C), 6 bars each = the full 3 cells × LUT × bus matrix.
Stacked sensor + inference against the 20 ms deadline. Double-column width.

**Honesty constraint baked into the code:** a bar is only split when
`sensor_ms + inference_ms` reconstructs the measured `e2e_ms`. For the three
LUT + 100 kHz runs the split falls below the 1 ms Timer_A tick and is *not*
resolvable, so those bars are drawn solid at the measured end-to-end value and
flagged `*` — no inferred split is plotted.

```latex
\caption{End-to-end per-sample latency with the sensor in the loop, against the
  20\,ms budget of 50\,Hz sampling. Each bar is the mean over 512 consecutive
  samples on the MSP430G2553 at 16\,MHz, timed in firmware; grey is the MPU6050
  read over I\textsuperscript{2}C and colour is one recurrent step. Real-time
  operation requires \emph{both} table-based activations and a 100\,kHz sensor
  bus: with LUT activations at 100\,kHz all three cells meet the deadline
  (12.01--13.98\,ms), and no other combination of the $3\times2\times2$ matrix
  does. At 10\,kHz the sensor read alone costs 8.4\,ms --- 42\% of the budget ---
  which pushes even the fastest cell past the deadline. This cost is invisible to
  inference-only benchmarks. $^{*}$At 100\,kHz with LUT the sensor/inference split
  falls below the 1\,ms timer tick and is not resolvable; the bar reports the
  measured end-to-end value without an inferred split.}
```
